Accent Contrast Test

Phase 1 acceptance artifact — the four member accents on both grounds at their real usage sizes.

Sizes under test — (a) body-adjacent labels: 0.75rem / 400 / mono / uppercase (13.5px at the site’s 112.5% base); (b) home commons lines: 1.05rem / 400 (18.9px). Ratios are WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance contrast; AA normal-text threshold 4.5:1.

Dark ground — #0f0f0f (the site’s ground)
